On 23.09.2026 the owner asked what width a storefront should be. I proposed measuring 13 live shops at 1920/2560 and adding a width option to the «Вид» panel. The owner snapped: «ты что не знаешь или поискать не можешь какой ширины делают интернет магазины».

**Why:** a widely published norm (content width 1140–1440, Shopify themes 1000–1600) needs no custom measurement. A measurement round costs the owner half an hour of waiting for an answer that is common knowledge.

**How to apply:**
- For general web and e-commerce norms, answer straight away from knowledge.
- Confirm with one quick web search and cite the sources.
- Keep live-shop measurement for what search can't answer, such as how the shops the owner named actually lay out a specific element.
- Don't turn every question into a new build item. Related: [[take-recommended]], [[rendered-checks-changed-pages]].
