On 24.09.2026 the owner looked at the product page and wrote «ритм воздух. тут все плохо. подключай скилы дизайнерские для исправления», then «и в целом дизайн говно везде, переделай скилами».

**Why:** my ad-hoc fixes (crumbs, image sizes, spacing tweaks) kept leaving monotonous rhythm and weak hierarchy. The owner judges by eye and expects designer-grade craft, not patches.

**How to apply:**
- For any storefront design change, the implementer invokes `impeccable`: context setup, then the right command (critique / layout / typeset / polish) with the spatial thesis written before editing. Add `redesign-skill` for an audit.
- Also invoke the kit's `scale`, `craft` and `shop` skills.
- The kit rules (roles, primitives, seams, no px numbers) win over the skills' defaults.
- Palette, font and the other Look choices belong to the owner, set in the panel. The critique targets composition, hierarchy, rhythm and details.
